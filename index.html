About the Doctor

Advanced Spine Care With Evidence-Based Treatment

Dr. Sri Krishna Chaitanya is a Spine Surgeon (Ortho) practising at KIMS Hospital, Kondapur, Hyderabad, with 16 years of overall experience — including 8 years as a specialist. His training includes fellowships in spine and neurospine surgery at Ganga Hospital, Park Clinic, and the University of Michigan.

A recipient of the AO Spine Asia Pacific Research Grant (2015) and the Indo-American Spine Alliance Fellowship Award (2016), he is dedicated to delivering compassionate spinal care — combining advanced surgical expertise with a patient-first approach.

How can I book an appointment?

Where does Dr. Sri Krishna Chaitanya consult?

What should I bring to my spine consultation?
Bring relevant scans and reports, previous prescriptions or treatment notes, a list of current medicines, and a summary of your symptoms. The doctor can advise if any additional evaluation is needed.
Will every spine condition require surgery?
Not necessarily. Treatment depends on your condition and assessment. Discuss suitable options with the doctor; an online page cannot determine an individual diagnosis or treatment plan.
